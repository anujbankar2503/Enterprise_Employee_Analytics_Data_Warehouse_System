# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
welcoming experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally.
- Focusing on the work and its impact rather than on individuals.
- Respecting different viewpoints, backgrounds, and experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private contact options
or by opening a confidential report with the maintainers. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled respectfully.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict project
participation when necessary to protect the community.
